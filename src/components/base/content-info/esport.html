Diverse eSports Betting Markets

Esports betting in Malaysia offers a wide range of markets to keep players engaged. Fans can bet on the match winner, choosing which team they think will take the victory. This is the simplest and most popular type of bet.

For those who like more detail, there are map-specific outcomes. Bettors can predict which team will win a particular map in a series. This adds an extra layer of strategy to betting decisions.

Another exciting option is first blood and first kill bets. These market types let players wager on which team or player will score the first kill in a round or match. It’s a quick way to add excitement early in the game.

Players can also bet on the total rounds or maps over/under. This involves guessing whether the total number played will be higher or lower than a set number. It’s ideal for fans who enjoy watching close matches or blowouts.

Lastly, handicap betting balances the odds by giving one team a virtual disadvantage or advantage. This makes betting fairer and more interesting when teams have uneven skill levels.
